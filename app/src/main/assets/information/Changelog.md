## 2026-10-22
- Music is half as loud as it was.
- The startup screen shows the carrot icon again, and its loading bar now fills smoothly instead of jumping and
  flickering.
- Sounds now pan gently left or right by where they are on screen.
- Sound settings now have Interface, Farm, Animals and Ambience, each with its own volume shown as a percentage instead
  of a switch (0% is off): animals (calls, petting, the cat's trill) are no longer part of Farm sounds.
- Opening the game shows the carrot app icon and a loading bar, then a circle opens onto your fully loaded farm; Reduce
  Motion uses a gentle fade.
- Every sound is balanced by type (animals a little louder than other farm sounds, fanfares a little louder, menu
  clicks and the night chime quieter, rain and music evened out) and all the volume sliders now start at 100%.
- Only animal sounds get quieter as you zoom out or look away from them (full volume only while zoomed well in, 40%
  fully zoomed out and never below 10%); every other farm sound stays at full volume wherever it happens.
- Animals you are zoomed in on and can see (not while they shelter inside for the night, rain or snow) call out now and
  then on their own, one kind at a time and not often; cats only ever trill.
- Settings has Timelapse: save a farm screenshot each in-game day at your chosen time, play or scrub through them with
  crossfade or instant transitions, check their size, clear them or turn recording off.
- Ducks lay eggs without eating, every 10 minutes (slower than hens), and the countdown for the next egg starts once
  you take it with the Bucket.
- A Chicken Coop holds 2 chickens at Level 1, 4 at Level 2 and 6 at Level 3.
- Fish in Storage have Release instead of Trash, and Storage's Equip button now says Select.
- Every Upgrade button just says Upgrade and opens a page with the details and the button that buys it; the Career's
  Home Upgrades are now Building Upgrades.
- The Tractor's countdown reads "Ready in" with the time left.
- The Cuddle Clouds song is gone.
- The Time sheet has preset buttons (Midnight, Morning, Midday, Rain, Evening, Night) instead of a slider, with a sky
  picture that matches the time; Midnight sets 12:01 AM and starts the new-day animation, and the other times no longer
  trigger extra new days, chimes or waves.
- Trees, rocks and ores that the new-day wave grows now fade in with a brief gentle glow in the wave's color.
- Tapping a farm animal now offers Release instead of Sell: it leaves for no money or XP.
- Releasing a butterfly has its own sound, and anything equipped from Storage shows how many are left after its name,
  in red as (x0) once you run out.
- The Storage buttons are wider, so Release has room around its text.
- Taking eggs, milk, wool or fertilizer from animals now earns XP, more for animals that unlock later.
- The Tractor's crops now give their XP when you store or sell the load, along with the cash from a sale, instead of
  when it picks them up.
- The new-day chime is tried again if it is dropped, so it plays every night.
- Butterflies can be caught with the Bucket for XP, kept in Storage, equipped and released back onto the farm, up to 10
  at a time; released butterflies stay, never leave and rest in one spot at night and in rain or snow.
- Butterfly wing colors in Career now count when you catch one.
- Every new day animation grows new trees, rocks and ores as the wave passes (5 to 7 trees, 2 to 3 rocks and an ore),
  spread evenly all the way round the map and mostly toward its edge, instead of them coming back while you are away.
- A fish you place never disappears, even if you let it go on the tile another fish started from.
- Petting or harvesting an animal no longer turns it or changes how it stands: it keeps walking, grazing or resting the
  way it was.
- Sheep are sheared with the Scythe instead of the Bucket.
- Farmland no longer has a cap: levels 2 to 19 unlock twice their number in tiles and every level from 20 on unlocks as
  many tiles as its number, for 657 tiles by level 30.
- Harvest quests never ask for more than 100 of a crop, and a second crop from the Scythe upgrade counts toward them.
- The fall sky is darker with a brownish-orange tint.
- Fireworks only go off when you level up.
- Paths and grass can go under buildings and decorations, and any terrain under a sprinkler.
- Paths cost $2 a tile; grass and farmland stay free.
- The new-day wave is green in spring, orange in fall and light blue in winter, and rolls out from the middle of your
  screen.
- Closing or backgrounding the app during the new-day animation no longer replays it or skips a day.
- The Axe takes fences down into Storage, and stored fences can be equipped and placed.
- The Tractor drives over farmland, finishes each connected plot of farmland before moving on, and can reach any ripe
  crop, even one in front of a pet house.
- The Tractor harvests only the ripe plants of a crop, and its Harvest button shows as soon as some are ready.
- The Tractor's load shows as a crop and count floating over it. Harvest it with the Scythe, which flies it to your
  totals like any other crop, or sell or store it from the Tractor's menu, at any time, even while it drives. It has to
  be emptied before the next trip.
- The Tractor makes a pickup sound when you are zoomed in close enough to see it work.
- Chickens and livestock step away from a driving Tractor.
- A crop tile feeds animals four times, and each meal removes one of the four crop pictures on the tile.
- The Tractor's load floats a bit higher over it, and eggs over hens are about 10% smaller and float a bit higher.
- Placing, destroying and cancelling use the words Place, Destroy and Cancel, and Storage rows say Trash.
- Button text is white on grey buttons, dark green on green buttons and dark red on red buttons in every menu.
- Removed the song Tales from the Vienna Woods.
- Dropdown list rows show how many you have placed out of the most allowed, such as Pet House (3/10) and Farmland
  (30/100), and prices in the Terrain, Decorate and Fertilize lists look like building prices.
- Sound, Graphics and Gameplay settings each have a Reset to Defaults button at the bottom.
- In the Build and Decorate lists, items that come in several looks or colors cycle through them; picking one still
  uses its usual look and the tool button does not cycle.
- Chicken Coops, Rabbit Hutches, Sheep, Goat and Pig Barns, Stables and Luxury Barns now start with 4 animals and can
  be upgraded with a purple Upgrade button to 7 and then 10, each upgrade unlocking at its own level from 7 to 24; the
  Career menu lists the levels reached.
- Leveling up opens a screen listing what the level brings: new crops, farmland tiles, buildings, decorations, trees,
  recipes, tool and building upgrades, new kinds of fish and when rarer fish become more likely.
- Added a Changelog to the About menu, first in the list. It has a search bar, a calendar button to pick a day or a
  range of days, days that fold away with only the latest open, and pages of seven days with Newer and Older buttons at
  the bottom. GitHub in the About menu is now called Source Code.

## 2026-10-21
- The Tractor finishes connected farmland before driving to separated farmland, and its menu lists crops by what they
  pay.
- Every harvest tool has two upgrades: the Axe fells trees in fewer hits, the Scythe and Bucket sometimes give a second
  item, the Pickaxe breaks rocks in fewer hits and the Fishing Rod widens its circle.
- Fish rarer than common ones become more likely every five levels up to level 30.
- Added the Career menu from the level panel, with collections for crops, fish, animals, buildings, decorations,
  terrain, recipes, environment and tools, charts for level and money, and search and filters.
- Finished quests are turned in automatically with a banner and the XP flying into the level panel.
- Every building is limited to one except Pet Houses, Chicken Coops, Sheds and Silos, and the Barn is split into Sheep,
  Goat and Pig Barns.
- Sprinklers can be set to spray all the time, and the first building of each kind sets off fireworks.
- Messages and banners appear in the middle of the screen.
- Terrain replaces other terrain and leaves fences standing.
- The Axe stores decorations, and any stored item can be equipped: decorations are set down for free, fish are released
  into water and crops are dragged onto animals to feed them.
- Storage rows show the name and count on one line with full-width Equip, Sell and Trash buttons.
- Animals that give something to harvest graze on their favorite crop in the field and give nothing until they have
  eaten, and stay fed until you harvest them.
- Pigs, rabbits and donkeys give only fertilizer, and hens hold one egg that floats over them.
- Moving a building or decoration only needs its bottom row on the map.
- Planting choices are named by what you plant, such as Potato Tubers.
- The Settings menu is a list of Load Game, Screenshot Farm, Sound, Graphics, Gameplay, Performance and About.
- Butterflies land on individual crop sprites.

- Uncommon and rare fish appear from level 10, epic from 15 and legendary from 20.
- Added the Tractor: a live overview of planted crops, harvest trips that drive over your farm, cargo you sell or store
  at home, and four facings.
- Added a Dynamic Shadows setting (off by default) with an intensity slider.
- Added pixel butterflies by day and wind curls past leaning trees; rain and snow lean with the wind.
- Music, Menu Sounds, Farm Sounds and Ambience each have a toggle and a volume slider.
- Added a Grid Lines setting and a Screenshot Farm button that saves your whole farm to Photos.
- Fish and ducks spawn on any water by its shape, and ducks can walk between ponds.
- The farm's clock holds still while the app is closed or in the background.
- Decorations can layer over each other by bottom row only, and dropdown items already placed as many times as allowed
  are greyed out.
- Item menus are ordered by unlock level.
- Animals are no longer held in by a roaming limit; fences are what keep them in.
