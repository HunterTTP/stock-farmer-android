Stock Farmer stores your farms and preferences on your device. It does not require an account and does not
send your farm, animal names, or settings to the developer.

The app contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics services. It does not request access to
your contacts, location, or microphone.

If you own stocks or open the Stock Market in your house, the app periodically (at most every five minutes
while the Stock Market is open) asks Yahoo Finance for the current price of the tickers you search for and
own. Those requests come from your device and contain only the ticker symbols, with the network information
any internet request carries; your farm is never sent.

If you allow notifications, Stock Farmer schedules crop reminders locally on your device. You can change
notification permission in your device's Settings. Music and sound effects can be turned off separately in
the game's Settings.

Recovery copies of replaced saves remain in the app's local storage; removing the app deletes its local
data. This Android app disables automatic app-data backups.
